SLOPE SURFER
Run, tuck into a ball to roll the hills, or launch and glide. One button controls gravity everywhere: hold for heavy, let go for light.

HOW TO PLAY

A/D or ←/→
Move left / right across the lane.
SPACE / TAP
Jump when running. Hold anywhere = heavy gravity (dig into slopes, dive fast). Release = light gravity (glide far, roll easy).
R / ROLL
Toggle Roll mode on the ground — tuck into a ball to surf the hills.
AIRBORNE
Any time you leave the ground you're Gliding — same hold/release gravity rule applies.
